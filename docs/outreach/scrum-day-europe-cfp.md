## Title

The Shape of Uncertainty: What Planning Poker Has Been Hiding for 25 Years

## Abstract

We've been estimating with single numbers for 25 years. A "5" from someone who has built this exact thing three times is not the same as a "5" from someone who opened the codebase yesterday. Same card, wildly different confidence. Planning poker hides this — and the conversation we actually need happens after the reveal, not during it.

What if the estimate itself showed confidence?

Skatting is a free, open-source tool where each team member drags a shape on a 2D canvas — horizontal for effort, vertical for certainty. High confidence produces a narrow spike. Low confidence produces a wide, flat curve. When the round reveals, every shape overlays on the same canvas, and the patterns are immediate:

- Three wide curves and one narrow spike? One person has done this before. Talk to them first.
- A tight cluster? The team genuinely understands this one. Move on.
- One outlier? Someone sees a risk the others don't. That's your most valuable conversation.

The question shifts from "why is yours an 8 and mine a 3?" to "what do you know that I don't?" — which is the conversation you actually needed all along.

In this session I'll run a live estimation round with the audience, show how the visual patterns map to real team dynamics, and discuss how 2D estimation fits into refinement, sprint planning, and roadmap conversations. The tool runs in a browser, needs no server or sign-up, and your team can use it tomorrow.

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand why single-point estimates hide critical information about team confidence — and what that costs you in refinement
2. Recognise the visual patterns that emerge when a team estimates with shapes instead of numbers, and know which ones demand a conversation
3. Experience a live 2D estimation round with the full audience
4. Walk away with a free tool your team can start using immediately — no setup, no accounts, no server

## Session Type

Talk (40 min) with live audience participation
